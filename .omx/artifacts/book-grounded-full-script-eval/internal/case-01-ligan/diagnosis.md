# Diagnosis: case-01-ligan

## Keep List

- Keep the short investigative form and the central discipline: say what the record confirms, then
  stop where motive is unknown.
- Keep every named person, event, quotation, and chronological relation.
- Keep the contrast between Su Jian entering formal judgment and Li Gan leaving no visible record of
  public adjudication.
- Keep `大将军匿讳之` and `鹿触杀之` as the two documentary anchors.

## Issue Classes

1. **Record-first opening**
   - Evidence: the second sentence immediately becomes `《史记》没有接着写审问，也没有写罪名`.
   - Reader cost: the opening reads like a research note before the human stakes are established.

2. **Repeated negative syntax**
   - Evidence: `没有接着写...也没有写...没有裁定...没有走...没有写...没有写...更没有...`.
   - Reader cost: repeated `没有` flattens emphasis and gives distinct evidence the same cadence.

3. **Weak relational transitions**
   - Evidence: the manuscript moves from Li Guang's death to Su Jian's case and then to the hunt by
     topic switch rather than by stating what each comparison can and cannot prove.
   - Reader cost: chronology remains clear, but the argumentative purpose of each fact arrives late.

4. **Abstract institutional wording**
   - Evidence: `史书没有裁定`, `李敢没有走这条路`, `没有从天子随驾的公开位置消失`.
   - Reader cost: procedural abstractions weaken a manuscript whose strongest material is concrete:
     a document, a blade, restraints, a hunting ground, and two four-character records.

5. **Ending restates instead of tightening**
   - Evidence: the last six lines repeat concealment, the hunt, and the official cause after those
     facts have already been delivered.
   - Reader cost: the ending closes twice and dilutes the contrast between known fact and unknown
     motive.
