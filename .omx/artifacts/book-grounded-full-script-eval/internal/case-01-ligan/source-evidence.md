  - Source signal: `删掉多余的信息与文字，突出重点` after first identifying the real point.
  - Supported move: combine repeated absence statements when they prove the same limit; preserve a
    negative only where its contrast carries new evidentiary weight.
- `copywriting-fundamentals` — `085 辞达而已矣`
