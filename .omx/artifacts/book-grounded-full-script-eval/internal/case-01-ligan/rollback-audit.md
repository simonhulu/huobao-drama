## Rollbacks

- Rejected an opening that called Li Gan `一个部下`; that relationship label was unnecessary and
  not stated in the frozen boundary.
- Rejected `本该审问、定罪、处分`; it would turn the comparison with Su Jian into a stronger legal
  expectation than the record supports.
- Rejected formal transition fillers such as `更值得注意的是`; the factual relation is carried by
  placement and the plain sentence `不久后`.
- Rejected extra hunting-ground atmosphere. The existing deer, scattered riders, bow, and shooting
  already carry the scene, and new detail would exceed the boundary.
- Rejected saying that the record gives no answer for Huo Qubing's motive, and rejected counting the
  closing records as `两句话`; neither claim was authorized by the frozen boundary.
