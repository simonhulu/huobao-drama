## Rollbacks

- Rejected mechanical connector upgrades such as `进一步来说` and `值得一提的是`; paragraph order
  now carries the relation.
- Rejected a full rewrite around a single new metaphor. The edit retains the baseline's doctrinal
  chain, quotations, old pine, medicine slip, and practical exercise.
- Rejected removing the no-luck, no-victim-blame, and no-merit-transaction guards for brevity; each
  protects the central viewpoint.
- Rejected combining the six quotations with their interpretations in long sentences; oral clarity
  takes priority over compact appearance.

## Boundary Result

- Six quoted passages and their attribution boundaries: preserved verbatim.
- Temple/seeing, mountain/stopping reinforcement, good action/turning insight into action: preserved.
- Required everyday examples: displaced anger, replayed messages, medicine slip, apology, repair,
  solitude, and bounded help all remain.
- Promise of instant luck, automatic erasure, or merit exchange: explicitly rejected.
- New factual, doctrinal, or causal claim: none retained.
- Source phrasing copied into candidate: none intentionally retained.
