# Diagnosis: case-02-three-places

## Keep List

- Keep all six quoted doctrinal anchors exactly and preserve their current attribution boundaries.
- Keep the three-part mechanism: temple as seeing, mountain as stopping reinforcement, good action as
  turning understanding into action.
- Keep the refusal to promise instant luck, automatic karmic erasure, or merit as a transaction.
- Keep the everyday examples of displaced anger, replaying old messages, receiving a prescription,
  apology, repair, solitude, and bounded help.
- Keep the practical close rather than replacing it with a new story.

## Issue Classes

1. **Overloaded opening paragraph**
   - Evidence: the first paragraph delivers the audience problem, the full three-part answer, a long
     quotation, a common misreading, a counter-question, and an instruction.
   - Reader cost: the listener receives the whole outline before there is room to feel the problem.

2. **Predictable corrective syntax**
   - Evidence: repeated `不是...而是...`, `更不是...`, `不是什么...`, and `不是说...` frames.
   - Reader cost: useful distinctions begin to sound manufactured because every point turns on the
     same grammatical hinge.

3. **Visible outline scaffolding**
   - Evidence: `寺庙为什么排在前面`, `第二个地方是山林`, `这就到了第三个道场`.
   - Reader cost: the structure is clear but reads as a lecture outline rather than a spoken line of
     thought.

4. **Competing metaphor load**
   - Evidence: account, legs, mirror, dead branch, nourishment, standing position, growing tomorrow,
     and transaction all appear in a short manuscript.
   - Reader cost: images are individually intelligible but do not accumulate into one memory.

5. **Generic example clusters**
   - Evidence: paired `有人...有人...` examples and three `可能是...` options compress people into
     illustration slots.
   - Reader cost: the manuscript names situations without letting one action carry enough weight.

6. **Dense spoken sentences**
   - Evidence: several sentences carry a quotation, interpretation, counter-reading, and application
     in one breath.
   - Reader cost: abstract nouns accumulate and oral emphasis has nowhere to land.

7. **Second ending**
   - Evidence: the practical paragraph already supplies a complete action close; the final paragraph
     then reopens doctrine, explains it, recaps all three places, and offers three symbolic choices.
   - Reader cost: the manuscript ends once in practice and again in summary.
