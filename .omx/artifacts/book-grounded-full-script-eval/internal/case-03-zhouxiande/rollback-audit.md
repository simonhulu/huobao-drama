## Rejected Changes

- Rejected adding a door-pulling gesture, ash on the fingers, waking with sweaty palms, falling
  incense ash, or a creditor knock.
  - Reason: these details could externalize emotion, but none occurs in the baseline or truth
    boundary; visual improvement cannot override the no-new-event hard stop.

- Rejected adding an explanatory aside about returning to debts and family at the moment of
  `放下`.
  - Reason: the boundary supports that interpretation, but the new aside did not repair one of the
    ten frozen issue classes and therefore failed the rollback rule.

- Rejected deleting the practical application section entirely.
  - Reason: diagnosis says compress, not delete; keep list requires practical application and central
    viewpoint.

- Rejected changing the teaching count to four.
  - Reason: user required preserving the three teachings; diagnosis flags the fourth item as a
    tutorial restart. 感恩 was retained only as a small supporting practice.

- Rejected removing the scripture anchors to improve pace.
  - Reason: user and boundary require four anchors and attribution boundaries.

- Rejected adding stronger causal language to explain the old friend's arrival.
  - Reason: would add certainty and violate issue 6/source move against false causality.

- Rejected replacing `频率` with a new metaphor.
  - Reason: title/promise depends on the narrator's bounded metaphor; issue 9 requires returning to
    the dry well, not inventing another conclusion.
