# Diagnosis: case-03-zhouxiande

## Keep List

- Keep the explicit legend frame and every named fictional event: ruin, the dry well, the journey,
  the three-day practice, return, debt repayment, and rebuilt livelihood.
- Keep the dry well as the principal story image and the monk's three teachings: awareness,
  reframing, and giving.
- Keep the narrator's disclosure that `频率` is a present-day metaphor and personal reading, not an
  ancient term, scripture, or scientific claim.
- Keep the four scriptural anchors and their present attribution boundaries.
- Keep the practical application section and the central viewpoint that repeated mental habits
  change how a person meets later choices and relationships.

## Issue Classes

1. **Scenes followed by explanatory sealing**
   - Evidence: after the porridge, the temple lesson, the three-day practice, and the rebuilt life,
     the manuscript tells the reader again what the scene means.
   - Reader cost: interpretation arrives before the scene's effect can settle.

2. **Too many governing images**
   - Evidence: dry well, clear/turbid air, stone, mud, waves, radio, station, dial, and oil all explain
     the same mental process.
   - Reader cost: no single image has time to gather meaning; the ending replaces rather than pays
     off the well.

3. **Tutorial repeats the story lesson**
   - Evidence: the monk teaches three practices in detail, then the narrator teaches them again and
     adds a fourth item.
   - Reader cost: after the narrative climax, the manuscript starts a second article.

4. **Mechanical enumeration cadence**
   - Evidence: `第一样/第二样/第三样`, then `第一件/第二件/第三件/第四件`, followed by another mapped
     list in the radio metaphor.
   - Reader cost: repeated list grammar makes the voice sound assembled even when the advice is
     useful.

5. **Generic emotional shorthand**
   - Evidence: `一眼看穿`, `像一直把他往那座山上引`, `真可笑`, `特别静，特别欢喜`, `浑身发麻`.
   - Reader cost: familiar labels announce feeling instead of letting the existing objects and
     actions carry it.

6. **Coincidence presented as proof**
   - Evidence: after practice, creditors soften, an old friend appears, and successive deals arrive;
     the sequence sits beside claims that fortune returned because the mind changed.
   - Reader cost: a legend's resolution risks sounding like a guaranteed causal formula.

7. **Metaphor slips toward pseudo-science**
   - Evidence: `频率就被它们压低了`, `频率不会真变`, `要提频率`, and the economic turnaround are
     linked after the one-time disclaimer.
   - Reader cost: the text can be heard as an objective mechanism despite defining frequency as a
     modern personal metaphor.

8. **Lecture-facing generalizations**
   - Evidence: `很多时候`, `总有人想`, repeated imperative `你`, and explanatory objections after
     the story.
   - Reader cost: the narrator leaves Zhou Xiande's lived line and begins instructing an anonymous
     audience.

9. **Late metaphor creates a new conclusion**
   - Evidence: the radio and dial arrive after the well has already been revealed and paid off.
   - Reader cost: the final image is conceptually neat but emotionally disconnected from the story.

10. **Pace imbalance**
    - Evidence: the journey and temple occupy the narrative center, but the post-story tutorial and
      radio explanation run for roughly another third of the manuscript.
    - Reader cost: the strongest scene arc loses momentum before the final line.
