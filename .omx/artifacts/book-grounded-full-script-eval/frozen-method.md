# Frozen Editing Method

This method is fixed before any candidate manuscript is written and is identical for all three
cases.

## Inputs

The editor may read only:

1. the frozen baseline manuscript;
2. its factual, conceptual, or fiction boundary named in `qualification.md`;
3. the bounded source excerpts recorded after diagnosis.

The editor must not load `polish-video-script`, an author imitation system, the rejected static
cards, prior style audits, a vector database, or another candidate manuscript.

## Procedure

1. Write a keep list before diagnosing defects. Passages without a concrete defect remain intact.
2. Name at most ten high-impact issue classes. Every issue must quote or precisely locate evidence
   in the baseline and describe the reader cost.
3. Search the four-book corpus only for those issues. Record at most three bounded excerpts for an
   issue, each with a book and locator. A source may teach a move; it may not donate prose.
4. Make a conservative edit of the baseline. Wording, sentence shape, paragraphing, order, opening,
   and ending may change. Facts, fictional events, title, central viewpoint, and source boundaries
   may not change. Final body length must be within 90-110 percent of the baseline.
5. Prefer exact verbs, concrete nouns, natural spoken syntax, and earned transitions. Do not make a
   sentence "more advanced" by mechanically replacing ordinary connectors with formal phrases such
   as `进一步来说` or `值得一提的是`. A connector changes only when its logical relation changes.
6. Run a rollback audit. Revert every material change that does not repair a diagnosed defect, is
   not supported by the recorded source move, adds a fact, strengthens a certainty, shifts the
   viewpoint, imitates source prose, or merely decorates an already working passage.

## Hard Stops

- No new named person, date, number, event, quotation, causal claim, or historical motive.
- No conversion of an explicitly bounded interpretation into fact.
- No copied source phrase of fourteen normalized characters or more.
- No title change and no body-length drift beyond ten percent.
- No model score may decide whether the method worked.

## Human Gate

The method advances only if all three candidates are publishable as-is, candidates win at least two
pairs and lose none, and no factual, viewpoint, imitation, or structural hard failure is found.
