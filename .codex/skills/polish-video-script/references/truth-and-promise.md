# Story Promise Contract

Load this before choosing a genre, retention structure, author module, or sentence move.

## Purpose

This contract protects the story promise. It is not a history paper contract and it does not ask
the writer to reconstruct a record precisely. Historical material is a source of characters,
events, conflict, images, and consequences. The writer may compress, reorder, combine, dramatize,
and invent connective material.

The hard questions are only:

- What does the audience expect to learn or feel?
- Who wants what?
- What keeps blocking that desire?
- What changes after each action?
- What result pays the opening promise?

## Selection Rules

1. Keep a material only when it creates desire, opposition, reversal, consequence, character
   pressure, a memorable image, or the final payment.
2. Delete a material that only proves research was done.
3. Prefer three to six load-bearing scenes over broad period coverage.
4. Do not preserve a dynasty, date, quotation, or example merely because it appeared in search.
5. If two materials do the same job, keep the one with the stronger conflict and delete the other.

## Writer Freedom

The writer may invent dialogue, interiority, sensory detail, compressed chronology, composite roles,
and connective scenes. These inventions must make the chosen desire, obstacle, action, consequence,
and emotional payment easier to follow. They are not required to carry source labels or archive
disclaimers.

## Story Logic Gate

Reject or return upstream only when:

- the central desire is missing;
- the next action does not follow from the current situation;
- an obstacle does not change the available choices, cost, or belief;
- two adjacent paragraphs perform the same job;
- the climax was not prepared by an earlier choice or pressure;
- the ending answers a different question from the opening;
- the manuscript can lose a paragraph without losing any story movement.

The final review evaluates story movement, emotional force, scene clarity, spoken fluency, and
promise payment. It does not evaluate historical precision.
