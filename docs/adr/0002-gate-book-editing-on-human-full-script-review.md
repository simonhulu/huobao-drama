# Gate book-grounded editing on human full-script review

Writing-book Markdown and generic search tools live outside `polish-video-script` as an offline
corpus. Book-derived editing remains an isolated experiment until a fresh three-script human blind
review produces at least two candidate wins, no losses, three publishable candidates, and no factual,
viewpoint, imitation, or structural hard failure. The earlier static-card runtime was removed after
its sentence-level evaluation failed to show a net quality gain; source coverage and automated scores
are diagnostic evidence, not permission to add production complexity.

## Recorded Outcome

The first issue-driven, book-grounded conservative-editing method failed the gate on 2026-07-31.
All three candidate manuscripts were rejected as unpublishable, with zero candidate wins; the
reviewer judged the baseline group stronger overall. The method remains outside production. The
same review also rejected all three baselines as unpublishable, so the underlying production skill
still requires a separately designed and freshly evaluated improvement.
